Partner Protocols

01

Official Bajaj Scheme Format

Bajaj Finance schemes follow the Tenure | Advance format (e.g., 24|0 = 24 Months tenure with 0 advance EMIs; 12|4 = 12 Months tenure with 4 advance EMIs). Each model variant lists its specific authorized Bajaj scheme matrix.

02

Fullswipe & UPI Qualification

Fullswipe and UPI cashback schemes apply when transactions are settled through authorized partner terminals (Pine Labs, Benow, Paytm, Innoviti, Razorpay).

03

HDFC LCE Special Rates

HDFC CC/DC/CL EMI Low Cost EMI (LCE) rates: 1.5% for Y Series, and 2.5% specifically for S2, X Series, and V Series models.
